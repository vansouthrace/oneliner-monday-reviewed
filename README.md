The funniest one-line review of my Monday, under 200 characters.
An entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-monday-reviewed-in-one-line-e55de3c1).

## Entry

> One star. Monday started with my alarm, peaked at the vending machine, and ended with me apologizing to a printer.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (validated to 1–200 characters, single line).

Run it with Node:

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this line

A real Monday in three concrete beats — alarm, vending machine, printer — with
the joke landing at the end. No generic tiredness, no meeting trope: just a
day that peaked at a vending machine and ended with an apology to office
equipment.
